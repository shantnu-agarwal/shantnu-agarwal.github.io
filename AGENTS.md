## Technology

Mobile-first, accessible, reusable components.

## Content rules
- Preserve approved copy and verified facts.
- Never invent metrics, articles or achievements.
- Keep the Indian state-government client anonymous.
- Never present placeholder images as personal photographs.
- Exclude phone, WhatsApp, GitHub and Instagram.

## Design
Follow the Editorial design system.
Prioritise reading, whitespace and typography.
Notes is a primary discovery destination.

## Workflow
Implement in phases.
Run tests, lint and production builds.
Do not deploy without approval.
