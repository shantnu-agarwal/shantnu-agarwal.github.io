
# shantnu.in — Project Brief

## Project identity

Website: https://shantnu.in
Owner: Shantnu Agarwal
Project: Complete redesign and content refresh
Design direction: Editorial Minimalism

## Vision

Build a lasting personal brand and publishing platform combining:

1. Technology and engineering
2. Product management and business
3. Writing, curiosity and intellectual exploration
4. Photography, travel and personal interests

The website must not resemble a conventional job-seeking portfolio.

It should feel like a thoughtful personal publication that also establishes the professional credibility of its author.

## Primary strategy

Content-led discovery.

The intended visitor journey:

1. Discover an article through search, LinkedIn or a reference.
2. Read something genuinely interesting.
3. Become curious about the author.
4. Explore professional work and personal background.
5. Reach out for an opportunity, collaboration or conversation.

Notes is the audience-acquisition engine.

Work establishes professional credibility.

About explains the person's story and perspective.

Life makes the person memorable.

Contact makes connecting effortless.

## Target audiences

- Recruiters and hiring managers
- Senior product and technology professionals
- Founders and entrepreneurs
- Business professionals
- Readers interested in technology, AI, products and strategy

Do not optimise every page explicitly for recruiting. The website should be valuable independently of career opportunities.

## Brand positioning

Engineering × Product × Business

Core identity:
An engineer turned product manager, currently pursuing a one-year MBA at IIM Ahmedabad.

Tone:
- Personal and confident
- Conversational
- Curious and thoughtful
- Technically informed
- Approachable
- Professional without being corporate

Avoid:
- "Visionary leader"
- "Thought leader"
- "Results-driven professional"
- "Passionate innovator"
- Generic motivational language
- Exaggerated claims

## Navigation

Logo: shantnu.in

Desktop navigation order:
Notes | Work | About | Life | Contact

The logo links to the homepage.

Notes appears first intentionally because publishing is central to discovery.

Mobile navigation must provide the same destinations.

2. A little about me
3. Selected professional work

5. Notes and writing

7. Contact invitation

The homepage should introduce rather than exhaustively explain.

## About page

2. Where it all started
3. From writing code to building products
4. Looking at the bigger picture
5. The things that keep me curious

## Work page

Purpose:
Demonstrate product, technology, business and leadership capabilities through actual work.

Include:
- Career timeline
- Three flagship projects
- Additional selected initiatives
- Capabilities
- Awards and recognition

Case studies should cover:
- Context and problem
- Personal role and ownership
- Approach and decisions
- Evidence-based outcomes
- Reflections

## Notes page

Purpose:
Primary content-discovery destination.

Editorial model:
Publication-led, mixed-format.

Content types:
- Quick Notes
- Perspectives
- Deep Dives

Categories:
- Technology & AI
- Products & Building
- Business & Strategy
- Life & Observations

Articles must support one primary category and optional tags.

Do not invent articles to populate the website.

When no articles are published, show a tasteful introductory empty state.

## Contact page

Purpose:
Make connecting easy while protecting privacy.

Primary:

Secondary:
"Prefer email? Reveal address"

Also include LinkedIn.

Do not include:
- Phone
- WhatsApp
- GitHub
- Instagram

## Long-term objectives

- Make the website an enduring personal identity.
- Build a body of original writing.
- Demonstrate professional credibility.
- Attract meaningful conversations and opportunities.
- Be easy to update without major redesigns.

## Success measures

Discovery:
- Organic search traffic
- Article readership
- Referrals from LinkedIn and other sites
- Returning visitors

Engagement:
- Movement from Notes to Work and About
- Article engagement
- Relevant internal-link usage

Opportunities:
- Genuine contact enquiries
- Professional introductions
- Recruitment and collaboration conversations

Do not introduce invasive visitor tracking.
