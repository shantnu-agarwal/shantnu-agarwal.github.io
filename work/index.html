Enterprise SaaS

Building an insurance platform for millions.

Senior Technical Product Manager
2025–2026

The challenge

Insurance processing involved complex operations and manual intervention. The challenge was to translate that complexity into scalable workflows.

My role

I led end-to-end scoping, planning, solutioning, development, testing and delivery of a government-sector enterprise SaaS product. The work involved more than 150 internal colleagues and stakeholders through demos, walkthroughs and training.

What changed

Manual claims verification and adjudication moved into workflows covering coverage limits, bill itemisation, medical case assignment and claims-processing rules.

Evidence

  • Approximately 7 million lives in scope
  • Approximately 2 million claims in scope
  • First software revenue for the government business division

It was an exercise in translating complex operational processes into technology while aligning people with varied responsibilities.

Consumer Products

Making insurance easier to use, at scale.

Technical Product Manager
2022–2025

The challenge

Insurance products combine policy configurations, customer journeys and operational rules in ways that can be difficult to understand and use.

My role

For approximately three years, I owned the primary policy-utilisation journey in the Bajaj Health app and website. I led a redesign across more than 800 configurations, built a localization framework for six Indian languages, enabled white-label capabilities and worked on reimbursement journeys.

How it operated

The product work included metric monitoring, alerts and production issue response, alongside the underlying journeys and integrations.

Evidence

  • More than 800 policy configurations supported
  • Initial localization framework covering six Indian languages

The growth figure describes the period of ownership; it is not presented as being caused entirely by these changes.

Business Transformation

Finding the business problem behind the technical problem.

Senior Technical Product Manager
2025–2026

The challenge

Incorrect or inconsistent insurance rules caused revenue leakage and manual payment adjustments, including around copayments and condition caps.

My role

I worked with business, operations and technology teams to identify the underlying rules and automate their application.

The outcome

Automated rule application reduced dependence on manual adjustments. The affected rules were relevant to approximately 80% of maternity claims daily.

No validated savings figure is presented here.
