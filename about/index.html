About

A little more about me.

An engineer by education, a product manager by experience, and a curious person by nature.

I'm Shantnu. I enjoy figuring out how things work, understanding why they work the way they do, and exploring ideas that catch my attention.

My interests have taken me in quite a few directions—from software engineering and product management to business strategy, photography, music, and technology.

Some of those interests became part of my career. Others have remained things I enjoy simply for the sake of it.

Here's a little about how I got here.

Where it all started.

I was born in Roorkee and grew up in Noida from around the age of six. I studied Computer Science and Engineering at SRM Institute of Science and Technology in Chennai, graduating in 2021.

At SRM, I was part of the Microsoft Student Partner community and led a campus team of 80 students. I also built a mental-health self-assessment app for SRM Medical Hospital on a pro bono basis, and explored voice-controlled web interfaces.

That exploration led to two research publications in 2021 and 2022. I enjoyed the whole arc: starting with an idea, understanding it deeply, and building something tangible.

From code to products.

I started my career at Bajaj Finserv Health in June 2021 as a software developer. I joined a small engineering team working on an incomplete sales application and spent time with business and sales teams to understand the problem beyond the code.

In June 2022, I moved into technical product management. Since then, I've worked across customer-facing digital experiences, enterprise integrations, insurance technology and large-scale software—bringing together engineering, design, operations, business and leadership.

I became a Senior Technical Product Manager in July 2025 and left in March 2026, after nearly five years.

Looking at the bigger picture.

In April 2026, I joined the one-year PGPX programme at IIM Ahmedabad. I wanted to develop a stronger understanding of the business context around technology, including economics, finance, marketing and strategy.

At IIM Ahmedabad, I have been involved with the Product Management Club and the Placement Cell as Head. The MBA adds another dimension to my work; it doesn't mean leaving technology behind.

Things that keep me curious.

Technology, gadgets and AI. Photography and videography. Flying a DJI drone recreationally. Playing the tabla and drums. Badminton and table tennis. Travel.

Things I've learned.

Stay curious.Understand the details.Make room for perspectives.

Still exploring.

There is always another question to ask, another system to understand, and another perspective worth making room for.
